### **Key for Columns**
- **Personal Data**: Name, address, employment, educational, financial, health, online activity, biometric/genetic data.
- **Data Processing Info**: Purpose, categories, recipients, retention periods, automated decision-making.
- **Personal File**: Incident reports, arrest records, interview transcripts, caution records, PNC records.
- **Body-Worn/CCTV Footage**: Audio/video recordings from Garda interactions or public/private cameras.
- **Exemptions (Gardaí/Courts)**: Law enforcement, ongoing investigations, informant identities, national security, judicial independence, legal privilege.
- **All Records/Files**: All documents containing personal data.
- **Names of Garda Officers**: Ranks, badge numbers, stations of all officers involved.
- **Access Logs**: Who accessed data, when, and for what purpose.
- **Third-Party References**: Witness statements, complaints, intelligence reports.
- **Format of Disclosure**: Permanent, intelligible, accessible format (PDF/printed).
- **Proof of Identity**: Requirements for ID verification.
- **Visits to Stations**: Dates, times, purposes, officers interacted with, CCTV footage.
- **Arrests/Custody Records**: Dates, times, locations, reasons, officers present, charges filed.
- **In-Person Events**: Identification parades, interviews, lineups.
- **Correspondence**: Emails, letters, faxes, internal memos.
- **Security Company Interactions**: Alarm triggers, dispatch requests, timestamps, outcomes.
- **Surveillance/Monitoring**: ANPR, GPS, social media monitoring, undercover operations.
- **Third-Party References (Detailed)**: Witness statements, associates, intelligence reports.
- **Administrative Records**: Retention schedules, deletion records, SAR history, complaints.
- **Cross-Border Data**: Mutual legal assistance, extradition, SIS entries.
- **Special Categories of Data**: Medical, ethnic, religious, sexual orientation.
- **Automated Decision-Making**: Risk assessments, predictive policing, AI tools.
- **PULSE Records**: Garda Síochána’s incident/case management database.
- **Fixed Charge Notices**: Traffic or other penalty notices.
- **Youth Diversion Programme**: Records from Garda Youth Diversion Programme.
- **Vetting Files**: Garda vetting records for employment.
- **GNIB Records**: Garda National Immigration Bureau records.
